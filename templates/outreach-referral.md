# Referral Outreach Sequence — 3-Email Framework

> A structured 3-email referral outreach sequence for prospects introduced by a mutual contact.
> Lead with the referral name to earn immediate trust. Send Email 1 within 24 hours of the intro.
> Replace all [PLACEHOLDER] fields with prospect-specific information.

## Email 1: The Referral Introduction (Day 1)

**Goal:** Leverage the referrer's credibility to earn immediate attention and a meeting request.

**Subject Line Options:**
- `[REFERRER_NAME] suggested I reach out`
- `Introduction from [REFERRER_NAME]`
- `[REFERRER_NAME] thought we should connect`

**Body:**

```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

[REFERRER_NAME] mentioned you'd be a great person for me to connect with —
they said [WHAT_REFERRER_SAID — e.g., "you're thinking about [TOPIC]"
or "you're the right person to talk to about [CHALLENGE]"].

[CONTEXT — briefly explain why you're relevant to them. 1-2 sentences.
Reference what [REFERRER_NAME] specifically shared about their situation.]

At [YOUR_COMPANY], we help [TARGET_PERSONA] [ACHIEVE_OUTCOME].
[ONE_SPECIFIC_PROOF_POINT — a result from a similar company.]

Given what [REFERRER_NAME] shared, I think there's a real opportunity to
[SPECIFIC_VALUE_FOR_THEM]. Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week?

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

P.S. [REFERRER_NAME] sends their regards.
```

**CTA:** Direct meeting request — referrals warrant a faster ask than cold outreach.
**Timing:** Within 24 hours of receiving the referral. Tuesday-Thursday preferred.

---

## Email 2: The Value Add (Day 4)

**Goal:** Provide genuine value while reducing friction for a busy prospect.

**Subject Line Options:**
- `Re: [REFERRER_NAME] suggested I reach out` (keep thread)
- `[RESOURCE_TITLE] — might be useful given what [REFERRER_NAME] shared`

**Body:**

```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

Just following up — I know [MONTH] is a busy time.

I wanted to share [DESCRIBE_RESOURCE — a report, framework, case study,
or insight directly relevant to their situation. 1-2 sentences.]

[KEY_INSIGHT — the most relevant takeaway for their specific situation.
2-3 sentences.]

Even if the timing isn't right now, I hope this is useful. And if a
15-minute call ever makes sense, I'm easy to reach.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

**CTA:** Soft — acknowledge their time constraints, offer value, leave door open.
**Timing:** Morning, their timezone.

---

## Email 3: The Graceful Exit (Day 10)

**Goal:** Respectfully close the sequence, update the referrer, and preserve the relationship.

**Subject Line Options:**
- `Re: [REFERRER_NAME] suggested I reach out` (keep thread)
- `Closing the loop, [FIRST_NAME]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

I don't want to fill up your inbox — this will be my last note for a while.

If [PAIN_POINT_OR_TOPIC] becomes a priority, I'd love to help. You can
reach me at [YOUR_EMAIL] or book directly: [CALENDAR_LINK].

I'll let [REFERRER_NAME] know I reached out. Hope we get the chance to
connect at some point.

All the best,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

**CTA:** Respectful close — leave door open without pressure.
**Timing:** Tuesday or Wednesday morning.

## Sequence Notes

- **Speed matters:** Referrals have a half-life. Send Email 1 within 24 hours of receiving the intro.
- **Always credit the referrer:** Mention [REFERRER_NAME] in every email — it's your trust anchor.
- **Warmer tone:** More casual than cold outreach. You have a shared connection — use that warmth.
- **Pre-sequence:** Obtain explicit permission from [REFERRER_NAME] before using their name.
- **Post-sequence:** Update [REFERRER_NAME] on the outcome — they'll appreciate the courtesy.
- **If they respond positively:** Drop the sequence immediately and move to natural conversation.
- **If they respond negatively:** Thank them for their time, update the referrer, add to nurture.
