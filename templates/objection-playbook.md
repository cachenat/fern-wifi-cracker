## Universal Objection Scripts

### 1. "It's too expensive" / Budget

**Underlying concern:** ROI unclear / no budget authority / comparing to wrong alternative

**FFR Script:**
```
I completely understand — budget is always a real consideration.

That said, this is something most of our customers brought up before
working with us. [SIMILAR_COMPANY] came to us with the same concern.

What they found was that the cost of not solving [SPECIFIC_PAIN] was
actually higher than our fee — specifically, they were losing [AMOUNT]
per [PERIOD] on [PROBLEM]. Within [TIMEFRAME], they'd recovered the investment.

Would it help to look at this through an ROI lens for your situation?
```

**ABC Script:**
```
That's fair — and I want to make sure this is the right investment for you.

Here's what I'd offer: the real cost comparison isn't our fee vs. $0. It's
our fee vs. what you're currently spending to live with [SPECIFIC_PROBLEM].
For [COMPARABLE_COMPANY], that was [SPECIFIC_NUMBER] in [WASTED_COST].

What would it be worth to your business if you could [SPECIFIC_OUTCOME]
in the next [TIMEFRAME]?
```

**Follow-up question:** "If price weren't a factor, would this solve the problem you need to solve?"

**Walk-away criteria:** No budget for 6+ months AND no trigger event on the horizon.

---

### 2. "Happy with our current solution" / Status quo

**Underlying concern:** Change is risky / current solution is "good enough" / sunk cost

**FFR Script:**
```
That's great — and honestly, if what you have works perfectly, I'd be the
first to tell you to stick with it.

Most of our customers from [COMPETITOR/SIMILAR_SOLUTION] said the same initially.

What they found, once they ran a side-by-side, was [SPECIFIC_GAP_IN_INCUMBENT].
The moment of truth was [SPECIFIC_SCENARIO_WHERE_INCUMBENT_FAILED].

Would it be worth a 20-minute comparison — just so you have a benchmark?
```

**Follow-up question:** "What would have to happen for you to reconsider your current setup?"

---

### 3. "Need to think about it" / Avoidance

**Underlying concern:** Not a priority / needs internal approval / something felt off

**FFR Script:**
```
Of course — this is an important decision and I wouldn't want you to rush.

That said, most of our customers who said they needed time found there were
usually one or two specific things they were unclear on.

What they found helpful was talking through those directly — it's usually
faster than going back and forth.

What specifically would you want to think through?
```

---

### 4. "Send more information" / Polite dismissal

**ABC Script:**
```
Happy to. Before I do, I want to send you the right thing — the generic
overview doesn't always answer the real question.

What would be most useful: a case study from a company like yours,
pricing details, or a technical overview?
```

### 5. "Timing isn't right" / Prioritization conflict

**FFR Script:**
```
I hear you — there's always a lot going on.

That said, a lot of our customers felt the same before starting.

What they found was that [SPECIFIC_PAIN] got harder to ignore the longer
they waited — specifically because [COST_OF_DELAY]. [COMPANY_X] told us
waiting 6 months cost them [SPECIFIC_OUTCOME].

If timing is the only thing in the way, when would make sense to revisit?
```

---

### 6. "Need to run this by my team" / Multi-stakeholder

**FFR Script:**
```
Of course — major decisions should involve the right people.

This is actually where we've helped a lot of our customers, because we've
been through this evaluation process before.

What they found useful was having us present to the broader team — everyone
hears the same thing and gets their questions answered in one conversation.

Would it make sense to schedule a 30-minute overview for the relevant people?
```

**Follow-up question:** "Who would need to be in the room for this to move forward?"

---

### 7. "We tried something similar and it didn't work" / Past trauma

**FFR Script:**
```
That's probably the most important thing you've told me — and I'm genuinely
sorry that experience happened.

A lot of our customers came to us after a failed implementation. They felt
exactly the way you do.

What they found was [SPECIFIC_DIFFERENTIATOR — implementation approach,
onboarding, or support model]. The difference in outcomes was [SPECIFIC_RESULT].

Would it be useful to walk through exactly how our process works —
so you can see what's different?
```

### 8. "Your competitor has X feature" / Comparison gaps

**ABC Script:**
```
That's fair — I won't pretend [COMPETITOR] doesn't do some things well.

Here's the honest picture: [COMPETITOR] does [X] well. We do it
[DESCRIPTION — differently or at parity]. Where we're genuinely stronger
is [SPECIFIC_CAPABILITY] — [PROOF_POINT].

The question is which matters more for what you're trying to do. If
[COMPETITOR_FEATURE] is your top priority, they may be the right call.
But if [YOUR_STRENGTH] is what you need, we're likely a better fit.

What's most important to you?
```

### 9. "We'll build it in-house" / Not-invented-here

**FFR Script:**
```
Building in-house is a legitimate choice — for some things, it's the right one.

Many of our customers started with the same plan.

What they found was that the full cost — engineering time, maintenance,
iteration, and opportunity cost — consistently ran 3-5x higher than expected.
[COMPANY_X] estimated [INITIAL_COST] before realizing they'd spent [ACTUAL_COST].

Would it be useful to do a quick build vs. buy analysis together —
just so you have the numbers before committing?
```

---

### 10. "I don't see the ROI" / Value disconnect

**ABC Script:**
```
That's fair — and if the ROI isn't clear, you shouldn't buy it.

Let me try a different approach. Instead of telling you the ROI,
let's figure it out together for your situation.

If you could [SPECIFIC_OUTCOME], what would that be worth to your
business in the next 12 months? [Let them calculate.] Now compare
that to [YOUR_PRICE]. Does the math work?
```

---

### 11. "Locked into a contract" / Switching costs

**FFR Script:**
```
Totally understandable — and I'm not here to push you to break a contract.

Most of our customers in a similar situation found the timing was actually
useful — it gave them 3-6 months to properly evaluate alternatives so
they weren't rushing at renewal time.

When does your contract come up? I'll put a reminder in my calendar.
```

---

### 12. "This isn't a priority right now" / Competing initiatives

**Follow-up question:** "What IS the top priority right now? I want to make sure this aligns — or know that it doesn't."

### 13. "No bandwidth for implementation" / Execution anxiety

**ABC Script:**
```
That's one of the most common concerns — completely valid.

Our implementation requires [X_HOURS] of your team's time over [Y_WEEKS].
Most of that is [LOW_EFFORT_ACTIVITIES]. [COMPANY_X] implemented us in
[TIMEFRAME] with a team that had less bandwidth than yours.

Would it help to see our implementation plan — specifically the hours
required from your side — so you can judge for yourself?
```

---

### 14. "How do I know this will work for us?" / Risk aversion

**FFR Script:**
```
That's the right question — I wouldn't expect you to trust me on my word.

Most of our customers felt the same before starting.

What they found was [RISK_REDUCTION_METHOD — trial, pilot, phased rollout,
money-back guarantee, reference call with similar customer].

What would it take to make you feel confident enough to try this in a low-risk way?
```

---

### 15. "Not interested" / Genuine disinterest

```
I understand — and I appreciate you being direct.

Can I ask one quick question? Is it that [SPECIFIC_PROBLEM] isn't a
priority, or is there something about our approach that doesn't feel right?

[If not a priority:] Totally fair. I'll reach back out in [X_MONTHS]
when things might be different.

[If approach issue:] I'd genuinely appreciate knowing what didn't land —
it helps me improve.
```

## Pricing Tactics

1. **ROI Reframing:** "Let's calculate what [PROBLEM] is costing you today..."
2. **Cost of Inaction:** "What happens if this problem isn't solved in 12 months?"
3. **Total Cost of Ownership:** Full cost of your solution vs. incumbent (including hidden costs)
4. **Scope Reduction:** "If full scope is too much, what if we started with [SMALLER_ENGAGEMENT]?"
5. **Payment Flexibility:** "What if we structured this as [MONTHLY_VS_ANNUAL / PHASED_PAYMENTS]?"

## Objection Prevention

Deploy these before objections surface:

1. **Price anchoring** — Mention ROI early so the price feels small by comparison
2. **Social proof seeding** — Reference similar customers proactively: "A company like yours..."
3. **Competitive acknowledgment** — Bring up competitors before they do: "You've probably looked at..."
4. **Timeline framing** — Establish urgency early: "Most companies in your position want this solved by..."
5. **Risk removal** — Offer trials or pilots before they ask: "We always recommend starting with..."
